This run diagnoses individual feature-label separation. It does not train a
detector and does not establish the multivariate contribution of a feature.

same episode are correlated. Episode-paired Wilcoxon results compare the mean
feature in attacked and normal UAV-steps within each attack episode, with
Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the four tests.
